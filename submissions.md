We are looking for essays that use economic history to make an argument: about money and banking, slavery and capitalism, the state and industry, or the history of economic ideas. Reviews, interviews, and replies to work published elsewhere are welcome.

## What to send

A pitch of a few paragraphs or a finished draft. Most essays run between 1,500 and 4,000 words. Cite sources with links or endnotes.

## How to send it

Email [{{ site.editor_email }}](mailto:{{ site.editor_email }}) with the pitch or draft and a line about who you are. Both editors read the inbox.
